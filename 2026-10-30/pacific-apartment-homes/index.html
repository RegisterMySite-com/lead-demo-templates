About us

A family company remodeling South Bay buildings

Pacific Apartment Homes, LLC and BD Management Company own and manage remodeled apartment buildings in and around the South Bay of Southern California and Santa Barbara. Our goal is to turn an existing building in a great area into a boutique place to live.

  • In-unit washer and dryer in many homes
  • Air conditioning in many homes
  • Pools, spas and fire pits
  • Rooftop and ocean-view decks
  • Online applications
  • Cats and dogs considered
