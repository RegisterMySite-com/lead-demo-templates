Pitch

APG Properties has a working site at apgproperties.com, a real license number in the footer, and nine current rentals. The problem is that a renter or an owner cannot trust the details that are supposed to close the lead.

The homepage asks people to take the company seriously and then misspells the offer: “Residental Property Management” and “Comprehensive Property Managment Solutions.” The stat row is blank until a script fills in 15 years, 300+ units, 800+ tenants, 70+ five-star reviews, and a 30-month average tenancy. Those numbers are not on the page as text, and 15 years does not match the About page, which says Shane Roach has owned APG since a 1996 founding and also that his property-management work began over 13 years ago. The California DRE record for corporation license 01525202 shows that license issued on October 12, 2009, with the main office at 28460 Brush Canyon Drive, Yorba Linda, a mailing address at the Euclid suite in Anaheim, and no current branches. The BBB profile says the business started November 7, 2008, and it is not BBB accredited. An owner comparing those pages does not get one story.

The office list has the same problem. The site advertises Anaheim at 675 N. Euclid Street #532 and Brea at 371 Oak Place, Suite H, both on (714) 400-9997. The DRE record does not list a Brea branch. BBB adds a Santee location at 10420 Avanti Avenue and three other phone numbers that never appear on the site. MapQuest lists a different Brea suite, 595 W Lambert Road, Ste 106. There is no email address on the pages a customer reads. The only email in the homepage code is info@apgproperties.com inside schema, attached only to the Anaheim address. The contact page has a form and a TTY number, and still no address a person can type into their own mail program.

The city pages read like unfinished templates. On the Buena Park page the heading is “The Importance Hiring Property Management Specialists.” The property types include “Single-family homes /mobile sq ft.” The areas-served close says the company works “throughout Anaheim County,” twice. That page also says the firm has been local for over ten years, which is a third timeline. About, contact, find-a-home, and the city pages we opened have no meta description, so the About link preview is the first lines of Phil Yates’s bio. The homepage uses four H1s, and one of them is just “Looking ?”. The usual sitemap addresses /sitemap.xml and /wp-sitemap.xml redirect to the homepage. The real sitemap index works, which means the site is one wrong URL away from handing Google the homepage instead of the page list.

The rentals are the part that should be clean, and they are not. Find-a-home and the AppFolio portal both showed the same nine homes on September 29, 2026, but the public listing URLs are AppFolio UUIDs, not addresses. Norwalk at 13217 San Antonio Drive is 1,872 square feet on the card and 1,900 square feet in the description. Anaheim Hills at 460 S Bridge View Drive is sold in the headline as a 4 bathroom home, then described as two full baths and two half baths. Photos are JPEGs on the AppFolio CDN, and the Norwalk exterior we opened is covered with a repeated watermark of the company name, license, website, and phone. Applications sit on Boompay and showings sit on ShowMojo, so the listing a prospect finds is not one page on apgproperties.com. A hidden “no vacancies” line is in the AppFolio HTML even while those nine listings are present. It is not what the visitor sees today. It is another sign the listing template was not finished.
