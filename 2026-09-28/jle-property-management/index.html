What is actually for rent

On September 28, 2026 their available-rentals page showed eight ads: Santa Ana (three), Anaheim (two), Buena Park, Whittier, and Riverside. Rents ran from $1,250 to $3,600. Square footage was published for only two of them.

Their homepage says the staff is bilingual and that maintenance is aimed at both apartments and rental homes. Each card on the board links out to Zillow for the live application. This demo keeps tour and apply requests on the page.
