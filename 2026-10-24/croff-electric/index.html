What we do

Residential & light commercial electrical work

From a new 240V outlet to rewiring a 100-year-old house, one licensed team handles the job from estimate to clean-up.

EV charger installation

Hardwired Level 2 chargers for your garage or driveway, plus conduit prep for future solar. Croff Electric is listed in SCE's Charge Ready Home contractor directory.

Panel upgrades & repair

Electric panel installation, repair and circuit breakers when you add big loads.

Rewiring older homes

Careful rewires in historic and older Long Beach houses.

Lighting & ceiling fans

LED retrofits, can lights, fixtures and ceiling fan installs.

Outlets, switches & 240V

New outlets and switches, plus 240V circuits for a stove, dryer or HVAC.

Repairs & inspections

Troubleshooting, electrical inspections, doorbells and generators.

Solar conduit prep

Run conduit now so a future solar install goes faster.
