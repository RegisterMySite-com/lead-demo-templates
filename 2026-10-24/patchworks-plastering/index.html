What Aston fixes

Small and medium repairs, done like they never happened

Patchwork's specializes in small to medium jobs, the holes and damaged patches that bigger crews won't take on or can't blend in.

01

Exterior stucco patching

After a panel upgrade, plumbing access, or removing a dog door or vent, the hole gets closed and blended in.

02

Lath & plaster repair

Interior walls and ceilings in vintage homes, on button board or wood lath.

03

Texture matching

Smooth, sand, lace, swirl and old-world finishes, including 75-year-old swirl stucco.

04

Water-damage stucco

Rebuilding damaged stucco on walls, garden walls and retaining walls.

05

Ceilings & walls

Refreshing or replacing plaster ceilings and walls, and plastering over hard acoustic texture.

06

Drywall & vent screens

Drywall repair, plus repair or replacement of foundation vent screens.

      FAQ

      Good to know

      How do I get a quote?

      Email aston.patchworks@yahoo.com with photos or video, as much detail as you can, and the city the job is in. Or call (714) 530-5119. Aston can bid from photos.

      Can you match my existing texture?

      That's the specialty. Reviews describe old swirl stucco and old plaster matched so well you can't find the patch.

      Do you patch stucco after a panel upgrade?

      Yes. Many reviews are post-panel-upgrade stucco patches, including scheduling around city inspections.

      Do you work on old lath and plaster homes?

      Yes, on button board and wood lath, walls and ceilings, and plastering over acoustic ceilings too.

      Are you licensed?

      Yes. California CSLB C-35 Lathing and Plastering #737480, active through 06/30/2027 and bonded.

      What size jobs do you take?

      Patchwork's specializes in small to medium jobs. For anything larger, send photos and Aston will let you know.
