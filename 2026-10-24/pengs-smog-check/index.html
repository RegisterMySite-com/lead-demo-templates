    FAQ

    Before you come in

    Do I need an appointment?

    No, walk-ins are welcome. Only motor homes need an appointment.

    Are you a STAR station?

    Yes. Peng's is listed as a STAR station on the Bureau of Automotive Repair's Auto Shop Locator.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 15 to 30 minutes.

    Do you do repairs if my car fails?

    No. Peng's is a test-only station. Get repairs done at a repair shop, then come back for the retest. Call for retest details.

    What should I bring?

    Your registration or DMV renewal notice, plus payment (cash or credit card). The check engine light must be off. On 2000 and newer vehicles, a lit check engine light is an automatic fail.

    Do you test diesel, hybrid, CNG or out-of-state cars?

    Yes, all of those, plus transfer-of-ownership checks.

    Call before you drive over
