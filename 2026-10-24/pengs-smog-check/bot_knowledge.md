## FAQs
- **Appointment needed?** No, walk-ins are welcome. Motor homes need an appointment, so call (562) 424-2368.
- **STAR station?** Yes, listed as STAR on BAR's locator.
- **How long?** Usually 15–30 minutes.
- **What do I bring?** Your DMV renewal notice or registration, plus payment (cash or credit card).
- **Check engine light on?** On 2000 and newer vehicles, a lit check engine light fails. Get it repaired at a repair shop first.

- **Electric car?** Fully electric vehicles don't need a smog check in California.
