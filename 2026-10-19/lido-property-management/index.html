    Applicant FAQ

    Quick answers for people applying to a Lido rental.

    How do I apply?

    Find the unit you like above and select Apply now. It opens that unit's own application on our AppFolio portal, where you complete and submit it online.

    How do I check my application status?

    Call the office at (714) 956-0235 and have the unit address handy. We will let you know where your application stands.

    How do I book a showing?

    Use the Schedule a tour form on this page, or call the office at (714) 956-0235 to set up a time to see the unit.

    Are the photos of the actual unit?

    Photos come from each AppFolio listing. Some listings note that their photos show a similar unit, and we flag that in More info.
