Renting with Brockman

From the office's own FAQ.

How to rent a vacant unit

  1. Pick a listing above and request a viewing, or call the office at (562) 597-0676 to set a time.
  2. Pick up keys at the office, upstairs in the back at 3720 E. Anaheim St., Suite 201 (between Termino and Redondo Ave).
  3. Apply online through RentCafe. The nonrefundable application fee is $40 per applicant over 18 and/or with a different last name.
  4. Approval takes about 24 to 48 hours. Move-in funds are paid by cashier's check or money order.

A purposefully small office

Husband-and-wife owners, long-term office staff, and an in-house maintenance crew. Current residents send maintenance requests through their RentCafe account.
