For owners: let us handle the rental

Leave the time-consuming rental duties to the team on Rosecrans. EXLNT's management service covers:

  • Advertising and vacancy showings
  • Rent collection and making payments
  • Routine property inspections
  • Preventive maintenance contracts
  • Resident screening (credit and eviction checks)
  • Handling all necessary repairs
  • Monthly financial reports, with year-end summaries
